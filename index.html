Request deletion of your HausHelp account and data

HausHelp creates a pseudonymous account on your device. You do not need an email address or password to use the app.

Request deletion

In the app, open your profile or privacy screen, choose the deletion control and confirm. If that control reports an error, deletion has not been confirmed. If you cannot use the app, email sundaytechventures@gmail.com to request deletion. Name HausHelp and describe the problem. Do not email passwords, access tokens or device secrets. We will explain how to verify that the pseudonymous account is yours before removing its server data. Without access to the device or a linked email address, we may not be able to match an email request to the account.

What is removed

Your HausHelp profile, memberships, reports, blocks and app-specific records are removed. Content you authored in shared conversations is anonymised or replaced with a removal notice so other members' replies can remain. Copies other people made outside the app cannot be recalled. Your data in any other SundayTech app is not deleted. A device-level anonymous ID may be retained only while another SundayTech app on your device uses it; otherwise it is deleted. Store subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Google Play or the App Store.

If the in-app flow is unavailable, use the email request above. We will not claim deletion is complete until we can verify the account and the server confirms the removal.
