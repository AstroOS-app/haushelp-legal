Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026. HausHelp is run by SundayTech Private Limited.

Data we handle

We create a pseudonymous account for each install, using Supabase. We do not ask for an email or password. This app uses display name, chosen community and country, recommendations and optional phone numbers you choose to submit, reviews, posts, replies, direct messages, reports and blocks. Public posts and recommendations are visible to other app members after moderation; direct messages and reports are restricted to participants or moderators. Store subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google; we receive purchase status, not card details.

Search questions of up to 400 characters and a session token are sent to our matching service to find relevant community content. Optional PostHog product analytics, if enabled in the build, receive events such as screen/feature use; search events may include a short query.

Your choices

Use the app's data-deletion control or request deletion on this page. The app can only remove server data after a verified session and confirmed server response. Your store subscription must be canceled separately. Some content copied by other people outside the app cannot be recalled.
